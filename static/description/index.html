Mercado Libre Publisher

Odoo 20 Community & Enterprise Odoo.sh & on-premise Support by Moldeo Forest

What it solves

The core of the suite: everything you need to run your Mercado Libre channel inside Odoo.

Publish and import products

Bulk publishing from Odoo to Mercado Libre, and import of your existing listings into Odoo. Images, variants with all attribute combinations, and SKU matching.

Orders, buyers and shipping

Mercado Libre orders arrive as Odoo sale orders, with the buyer, the shipping information, pack orders, and the sale fee and shipping cost.

Automatic synchronization

Stock and prices synchronized in bulk and on schedule. Orders in real time through Mercado Libre notifications. Buyer questions inside Odoo.
